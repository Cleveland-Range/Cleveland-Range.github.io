Milestones

Discover our history.

24 milestones from 1847 to 2022.

1847

Original company started in Cleveland as a small retail store manufacturing the “French Steel Range”

1890

The company becomes the Born Range Company and moves to Galion, Ohio.

1898

Born Range moves back to Cleveland, Ohio.

1922

The food service division separates from Born Range and becomes Cleveland Range delivering the first stainless steel construction, the first stainless steel steam cooker, the first units with thermostatic traps, synchronized with pressure relief valves, the first removable aluminum doors, the first copper jet boilers and the first atmospheric steam generators.

1974

Introduced the first pressureless convection steamer, The Cleveland Convection Steamer™, model D.

1979

Introduced the first Counter Top Pressureless Steamer, the SteamCraft™ II.

1982

Introduced a complete line of steam-jacketed kettles and tilting skillets.

1987

Introduced the first combination pressure/pressureless steamer, the SteamPro™.

1988

Introduced the first high capacity counter top pressureless convection steamer, the SteamCraft V™.

1990

Introduced the first high capacity convection steamer, the ConvectionPro™.

1991

Introduced a complete line of gas & electric Combi steamer/ovens, the CombiCraft™.

1992

Introduced first steamer with power burner the SteamCraft™ Ultra 10

1993

Introduced first vertical steam boiler in a steamer, the SteamCraft™ Power 10.

1996

Introduced the first Pump Descaling System.

1997

NickelGuard Boilers became standard in many models.

1998

SteamCub Boilers Steamer and Gemini Steamer Introduced.

2001

5 Year 100% Parts and Labor warranty standard on NickelGuard

2002

Introduced the Patented SteamChef 3 Boilerless Convection Steamer with Kleanshield

2005

Introduced Convotherm by Cleveland Combi Oven Steamers Built in Cleveland, Ohio

2008

Cleveland Range acquired by Manitowoc

2015

Manitowoc spinoff- Welbilt, Cleveland Range becomes a Welbilt Company.

2018

Cleveland Range begins to manufacture steamers in Toronto facility.

2019

Cleveland Range undergoes a product refresh with updated controls.

2022

Cleveland Range/Welbilt is acquired by Ali Group, as well as a rebranding to the current logo.

Facilities

Two locations in North America.
