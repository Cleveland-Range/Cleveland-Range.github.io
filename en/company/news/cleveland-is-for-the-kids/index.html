Cleveland is For The Kids

Advocating for the Future of School Meals, and Creating Quality K12 Equipment to Support Kitchen’s Needs

Last week, our team proudly represented Cleveland at the School Nutrition Association (SNA) Legislative Action Conference on Capitol Hill. With over 850 dedicated professionals in attendance, the event was a powerful testament to the importance of ensuring that every child has access to free, nutritious school meals.

The Fight to Keep School Meals Free

One of the major topics of discussion was the ongoing effort to keep school lunches free for all students. Research shows that school meals are the healthiest meals many children receive in a day, fueling their academic success and supporting millions of working families across America. However, proposed federal funding cuts threaten the sustainability of these vital programs.

Key Takeaways from the Conference

  • Panel Discussions & District Updates: Experts, school nutrition professionals, and policymakers engaged in insightful discussions on the challenges and successes of current school meal programs.
  • The Impact of Federal Budget Cuts: Many school districts provided updates on how reductions in federal funding are making it harder to serve nutritious meals. The increased burden of paperwork also creates barriers, leading to food insecurity for both students and school nutrition departments.
  • A Call to Action: SNA is urging Congress to increase federal reimbursements by $0.40 per lunch and $0.15 per breakfast to help schools keep meals accessible and nutritious for all students.

Cleveland is for the Kids

As a company deeply invested in school nutrition, we take pride in serving districts that make student well-being a priority. We have received positive reviews from several school districts, including Fulton County, showcasing the impact of quality, nutritious meals on students’ success.

How You Can Help

We encourage everyone, parents, educators, and community members, to advocate for the protection of school meal programs. Contact your legislators and urge them to support increased school meal reimbursements. Together, we can ensure that every child, regardless of financial background, has access to free breakfast and lunch at school. Cleveland is for the kids, and we are proud to stand with SNA and school nutrition professionals in this fight. We will continue to support policies that protect and strengthen school meal programs. Let’s work together to ensure that no child goes hungry at school!
