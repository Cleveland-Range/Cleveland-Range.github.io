SGL40T1 easyTouch: PowerPan Series Braising Pan & Tilting Skillet

Available in easyTouch or easyDial Controls, Contact Team for More Info

SGL40T1 easyTouchSpec SheetCAD/RevitFind a RepRequest a Quote

New easyTouch 7″ UI interface streamlines operations with an intuitive touch display and a variety of cooking modes. Key features include a Product Temperature Probe with the ability to control temperature probe ±1°F, Press & Go Recipes with built-in cookbook storage, Multi-Step Cooking with option for customizable messages between cook stages, and Self Diagnostics & Maintenance for real-time error and event logging.

Anatomy

Built to work a full shift.

  1. 01

    Cooking Surface

    5/8″ Stainless Steel Bead Blasted cooking surface prevents warping and keeps food from sticking.

  2. 02

    Pan Construction

    Durable 12 gauge, 304 Stainless Steel pan construction. 5/8″ (16mm) mild steel clad bottom plus a 1/16″ (1.6mm). Stainless Steel plate for even temperature distribution.

  3. 03

    Low Height

    Low 35″ rim height for easy operation and cleaning.

  4. 04

    7″ easyTouch Control

    easyTouch 7″ UI display interface is conveniently located in a Drip Proof Side Console. Temperature controlled up to 425°F (220°C). New easyTouch UI interface simplifies operations with accurate touch tune and various cooking modes, including Product Temperature Probe with the ability to control temperature probe ±1°F, Press & Go Recipes with cookbook storage, Multi-Step Cooking with option to add message between cook stages, and Self Diagnostics & Maintenance for error and event logging.

  5. 05

    Safety Tilt Feature

    Easy-to-use power tilt with manual override and enclosed permanently lubricated gearbox.
