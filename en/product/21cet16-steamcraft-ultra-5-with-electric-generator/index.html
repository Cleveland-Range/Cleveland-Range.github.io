21CET16: Steamcraft Ultra 5 with electric generator

COUNTER TYPE DESIGN, PRESSURELESS CONVECTION STEAMER, ELECTRIC STEAM GENERATOR, 16 KW CLEVELAND, SteamCraft‚ Ultra 5, one compartment, Counter-Type Steamer, Model 21-CET-16, heavy duty all Stainless Steel construction. Rear mounted, insulated Steam Generator with Remote Probe Type Water Level Controls and Automatic Steam Generator Blowdown with “Water Jet” Drain Cleaning feature.

Anatomy

Built to work a full shift.

  1. 01

    Left Hand Door Hinging

    Compartment Doors hinged left, controls on the right.

  2. 02

    Steam Distribution

    Exclusive Steam Cooking Distribution System: Exclusive Brass Steam Jets produce a high velocity convection steam without fans. Coved Corner design in cooking compartment distributes heat evenly, and is easy to clean. Creased top & bottom enhance drainage. Cold Water Condenser for each compartment maintains a dry steam. Fully Insulated cooking compartment for thermal efficiency. Removable Stainless Steel Slide Racks.

  3. 03

    Generator Drain

    Automatic Generator Drain: Contains a “Water Jet” Spray Rinse Drain Cleaning Cycle to keep drain clear.

  4. 04

    Controls

    One 60 Minute Electro-Mechanical Timer and Switch for manual operation. Audible signal for cooking time completion. Separate Switch for Power “ON/OFF”.

  5. 05

    Steam Generator

    Innovative PowerPak Electric Steam Generator with one Special Stainless Steel Fire Bar Heating Element. Strong 14 Gauge Stainless Steel Construction. Large 3.5 gallon generator reservoir for fast steam cooking production. Fully insulated rear mounted steam generator.
