Do Not Track (DNT) is a privacy preference that users can set if they do not want web services to collect information about their online activity. We do not respond to DNT signals, with the exception of Global Privacy Controls and Universal Opt-Out Mechanisms as explained above.

Use of Cookies, Pixels, and Other Tracking Technologies

Our website may store or retrieve information on your browser, mostly in the form of cookies. A cookie is a small piece of data (text file) that a website places on your device to remember information about you, such as your language preference or login information.

We use first-party cookies primarily to make the website work as you expect it to — for example, to allow you to navigate between pages efficiently, analyze how well our website is performing, and understand the content you spent the most time reviewing. In some cases, we use first-party cookies for targeted advertising.

We also incorporate third-party cookies and similar technologies, such as pixels, tags, and web beacons. Third-party cookies gather information to enable our vendors to provide a range of services to us, including targeted advertising and measuring the success of our advertising campaigns.

You can prevent the collection of data by non-essential performance, functional, and marketing cookies by managing your cookie preferences through your browser settings or by contacting us at dataprivacy@welbilt.com.

How We Use Cookies

  • Provide you with services available through the website and to enable you to use some of its features;

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you which amount to a request for services, such as setting your privacy preferences, logging in, or filling in forms.

Non-Essential Cookies

Non-essential cookies serve purposes beyond core website functionality and fall into three subcategories:

  • Performance cookies – collect information about user behaviour on the website without collecting personal information (e.g., pages visited, ads viewed).
  • Functional cookies – track and remember user preferences and past choices on the website to provide a personalized experience (e.g., usernames, regions).
  • Targeting cookies – can track content you view, links you follow, and your browser/device information and IP address, and may be used to build user profiles or display advertisements.
